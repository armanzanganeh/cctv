These controls apply to every connected camera. Motion, sound, and optional people detection run on the camera phone. Telegram can report camera online/offline, network changes, battery and charger status, camera warnings, occupancy changes, security events, and recovered footage. AI occupancy uses COCO-SSD locally; the first use downloads the model.

The bot token is stored in this browser's local storage. For a public product, move it to a protected backend. Offline clips are stored temporarily on the camera phone and transferred when the dashboard reconnects. Existing per-camera Telegram settings in index.html are left intact and continue to work.
